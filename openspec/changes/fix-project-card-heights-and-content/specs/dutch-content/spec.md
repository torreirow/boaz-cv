## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Dutch content is concise and natural

The Dutch content in data/nl/ YAML files SHALL use clear, concise, and natural language appropriate for a teenage student's portfolio.

#### Scenario: About section content

- **WHEN** viewing the about section in Dutch
- **THEN** descriptions SHALL be concise without unnecessary detail
- **AND** language SHALL sound natural and age-appropriate
- **AND** school, sports, work, and interests SHALL be clearly described

#### Scenario: Achievements section content

- **WHEN** viewing achievements in Dutch
- **THEN** achievements SHALL focus on skills and teamwork rather than statistics
- **AND** descriptions SHALL emphasize learning and growth
- **AND** tone SHALL be modest and authentic

#### Scenario: Education section content

- **WHEN** viewing education in Dutch
- **THEN** timeline SHALL show current status ("heden" for ongoing)
- **AND** extracurricular activities SHALL be listed concisely
- **AND** school program details SHALL be clear

#### Scenario: Experiences section content

- **WHEN** viewing experiences in Dutch
- **THEN** sports team name SHALL match official organization name
- **AND** work responsibilities SHALL be clearly described
- **AND** dates SHALL be accurate and current

#### Scenario: Projects section content

- **WHEN** viewing projects in Dutch
- **THEN** projects SHALL be ordered logically (current website, future school project, hobby)
- **AND** timeline SHALL be accurate ("2026" for current year)
- **AND** descriptions SHALL be clear and concise
